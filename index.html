Move to a new phone

Move your log, reasons and settings to another device. Show the QR code, scan it with the new phone's camera, open the link, then tap Import.

App lock

Protect Unplugged with a PIN. It asks for the PIN every time you open the app.

Lock the app

This is a privacy screen to keep casual snoopers out. Your log isn't encrypted. If you forget your PIN, the only way back in is to erase everything.

Your data

Your log lives only in this browser. Export a backup before clearing your browser data or switching devices.

Install the app

Add Unplugged to your home screen for a full-screen app with no browser bar. It works offline too.
